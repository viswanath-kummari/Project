The Problem Statement

Why Traditional Retail Software Fails Meat Shops

Butcher shops operate on perishable goods, continuous weight variations, and rapid price fluctuations that standard supermarket software cannot handle.

Decimal Weights vs Piece Counts
Generic POS software only counts items in integers. Meat shops sell cuts in fractional decimals (e.g. 1.345 kg) alongside whole items (e.g. 30 eggs).
Spoilage & Trimming Wastage
De-boning, fat trimming, and cold-chain fluctuations cause 5%–12% unrecorded shrinkage, silently wiping out retailer profit margins.
Long Peak-Hour Counter Queues
Sunday morning rushes require 10-second checkout speeds. Complex billing interfaces create bottlenecks and impatient customer drop-offs.
Catalog Compliance & Auditing
Strict regional dietary compliance demands guaranteed isolation (e.g. zero-beef catalog adherence) and rigorous cashier audit logs.
Presenter Script: "Traditional POS and ERP platforms are built for packaged groceries. Meat shops face three unique hurdles: fractional decimal weights, high shrinkage from carcass trimming and cold-chain shelf-life, and extreme Sunday morning counter traffic. FRESH CUT was purpose-built to conquer every one of these pain points."

Module 1: High-Speed POS

Built for Butcher Counter High-Traffic Rushes

Precision dual-unit billing (KG decimals & Piece counts) with multi-tender payment processing.

Dual Unit Logic
• KG Weight Items: Support precise 3-decimal values (e.g. 1.250 kg Chicken Breast).
• Piece Items: Whole number quantities for eggs, whole crabs, and quail.
• Quick dial buttons (+0.25kg, +0.5kg, +1.0kg) for single-tap cart updates.
Multi-Tender Payments
• Cash: Tendered amount input with instant change calculation.
• UPI / QR Code: Interactive dynamic payment modal with sound cue.
• Card & Credit: Supports customer accounts and split bills.
Thermal Receipt Engine
• Formatted for standard 80mm & 58mm thermal printers.
• Displays Store Name, Viswanath (Admin), Hemanth (Cashier), itemized weights, tax breakdown, and QR verification.
Presenter Script: "Our POS billing counter was designed for maximum speed. Hemanth can select an item, tap the quick dial weight chips, review the live tax calculation, accept cash or UPI via dynamic QR codes, and trigger a formatted 80mm thermal receipt in under 8 seconds."
Module 2: Inventory Control

Stock Intake & Automated Shrinkage Control

Prevents negative stock, records wholesale intake, and forces accountability on meat trimming loss.

+ Add Stock Intake Modal
• Dedicated stock increment modal with quick presets: +0.5 kg to +50 kg or +5 to +100 pcs.
• Live projected balance box shows existing stock, incoming delivery, and new balance before committing.
Spoilage Audit Logging
• Mandatory loss categorization: Trimming Loss, De-boning Scrap, Cold-Chain Fluctuation, Shelf Expiry.
• Links wasted weight directly to cost price to deduct from gross profit automatically.
Real-Time Movement Ledger
• Every transaction (Sale, Intake, Spoilage, Manual Adjustment) is permanently logged in the movement ledger with timestamp, user ID, and batch reference.
Presenter Script: "Inventory control is where meat shops either thrive or lose money. We introduced an intuitive '+ Add Stock' intake modal that lets managers increment inventory in seconds, while our spoilage logger requires cashiers to categorize meat waste so shrinkage is never hidden."
